Complex plane · modular fingerprints

Each point represents the exact residue of a reversed π prefix modulo a prime. Complex phases reveal divisibility; phase plots alone do not prove primality.

Roots-of-unity wheel

Gold = most recent prefix; teal = nonzero residues; pink = divisible prefixes (residue zero). Consecutive prefixes share digits, so the points are dependent.

Divisibility heatmap

Columns are prefix lengths from the currently selected recent window. Rows are prime moduli. Bright cells mean an exact divisor of the corresponding Fundamental integer; they do not establish compositeness when the integer equals that prime.
